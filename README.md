# 🛒 Grocery Sales and Revenue Analytics using Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

**Grocery Sales and Revenue Analytics** is a Power BI-based business intelligence project designed to analyze grocery retail sales, revenue, product, brand, inventory, customer, and store-level performance.

The project transforms raw Indian FMCG retail data into an interactive dashboard that helps identify sales trends, high-performing products and brands, revenue patterns, inventory conditions, and actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Grocery retailers generate large volumes of sales and inventory data, making it difficult to identify important business trends through raw data alone.

This project addresses the following business questions:

* What are the overall sales and revenue performance levels?
* Which products and brands generate the highest revenue and margin?
* Which cities and store formats perform best?
* Which categories contribute most to revenue?
* How does sales performance vary across different channels?
* What are the inventory and stock-on-hand patterns?
* What business actions can be recommended based on the analysis?

---

## 📊 Dataset

**Dataset:** `Indian FMCG Retail Sales  Customer  Inventory (2024).csv`

The dataset contains Indian FMCG/grocery retail transaction and inventory information, including fields related to:

* Invoice details
* Invoice date
* City
* Store format
* Product category
* Brand
* Sales channel
* Payment mode
* Customer demographics
* Loyalty information
* Quantity
* Revenue
* Cost
* Margin
* Inventory / Stock on Hand

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared using **Power Query in Power BI**.

Key data-preparation activities included:

* Data type verification
* Missing-value identification
* Error-value checking
* Category and brand validation
* Date-field preparation
* Numerical-field validation
* Identification of `Unknown`/missing categories
* Data-quality assessment
* Creation of analytical fields and measures

The data-quality analysis was performed before building the final dashboard to improve the reliability of the business insights.

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

* Top-performing brands and products
* Revenue and margin leaders
* Strong and weak-performing cities
* Category-wise revenue contribution
* Store-format performance differences
* Channel-wise sales patterns
* Inventory and stock-on-hand conditions
* Areas requiring business attention

The dashboard converts these findings into actionable recommendations for improving sales, profitability, product strategy, and inventory management.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis, businesses can:

1. **Prioritize high-performing brands and products** to maximize revenue and margin.
2. **Improve underperforming product categories** through targeted promotions and pricing strategies.
3. **Focus on high-performing cities and store formats** when planning expansion.
4. **Optimize inventory levels** to reduce stock-out and overstock risks.
5. **Strengthen effective sales channels** based on channel-wise performance.
6. **Use margin analysis alongside revenue** to make more profitable product decisions.
7. **Monitor business KPIs regularly** through the Power BI dashboard.
